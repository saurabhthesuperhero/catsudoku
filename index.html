Cat Sudoku

One cat per row, column and colour. Cats never touch.

A cozy logic puzzle: tap a cell once for a paw mark, again for a cat. Every board has exactly one answer and can be solved with pure logic, no guessing.

  • 1000 levels from 4×4 to 11×11, then endless puzzles
  • A new daily puzzle with a streak calendar
  • Hints that explain the next logic step
  • 12 cats to collect and a pet cat to feed
  • Your records: beat last week, best times per board size
